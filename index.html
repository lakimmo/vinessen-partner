FAQ

Common questions

Is Vinessen a video-tech company or a wine distributor?

We're a wine distributor first - 20+ years sourcing and exporting from independent producers across Europe, Australia, Africa, North America, and Asia. The QR-code/video concept is something we've built on top of that, not instead of it. You're still working with a distributor; this is simply how a growing part of our catalog now comes to life in the bottle.

Do the winemaker videos cost extra?

No. The QR code and video are included in the price of the bottle - there's no licensing fee, no per-scan cost, nothing added to your invoice.

Do all 240 references come with a video?

Not yet. 51 references already have their own winemaker video today, and 90+ more are in production. The rest remain available exactly as a normal wine order.

Can I choose which references get the QR code, or is it fixed?

Yes, it's up to you. Not every reference needs to launch with a QR code from day one. Many importers start by testing it on just a handful of references, see how their customers respond, then expand from there.

Do I need to change anything on my end - packaging, import process, customs?

No changes needed. The QR code is printed directly on the front label before the bottle ships to you - it's imported, stored, and sold exactly like any other bottle.

What will this concretely bring me?

For you: a disruptive offer in a category where everything looks alike, a concrete argument when selling in to stores and retailers, access to 240+ references with no minimum order, a reason for your customers to come back for your range specially, boosted sales, and a way to speak to a younger generation at ease with scanning and video.

For your end customers: a tasting guided by the winemaker themselves, no jargon, accessible in 3 seconds with no app to install, wine made approachable with no expertise needed, available 24/7 automatically in their own language, and a bottle that becomes a gift with meaning.

What languages are the videos available in?

Videos are automatically subtitled in French, English, Russian, Thai, Portuguese, Vietnamese, German, Chinese, Spanish, Korean, Arabic, Polish, and Italian.
